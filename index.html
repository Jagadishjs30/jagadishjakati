About

Academic Profile

Dr. Jagadish S. Jakati is a Senior Assistant Professor with over 13 years of dedicated academic and research experience in engineering education. Holding a Ph.D. in Speech Processing from VTU Belagavi, he brings deep expertise in signal processing, machine learning, and embedded systems.

His research output spans 13+ Scopus and WoS indexed journal publications, 6 patents, and international conference presentations — demonstrating a sustained commitment to advancing knowledge in speech enhancement, biometrics, IoT, and deep learning for real-world applications.

Beyond research, Dr. Jakati has held key administrative roles including Dean of R&D and ISO Certified Internal Auditor, reflecting his broader contributions to institutional growth and governance.
